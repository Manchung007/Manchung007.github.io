About

This website presents a curated record of peer-reviewed research publications spanning 2013–2025 in Nature Photonics, Nature Communications, Journal of the American Chemical Society, Angewandte Chemie International Edition, and Advanced Materials.

The work focuses on molecular and materials design for efficient, stable organic light-emitting devices, with related contributions in photovoltaics, photonics, and energy-storage materials.

Research Areas

  • Organic light-emitting diodes and operational stability
  • Gold(III), platinum(II), and nickel(II) luminescent complexes
  • Thermally activated delayed fluorescence and phosphorescence
  • Organic optoelectronic materials and molecular photophysics
  • Nanophotonics, organic photovoltaics, and electrochemical energy storage
